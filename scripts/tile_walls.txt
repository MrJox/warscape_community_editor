# Procedural walls, phase 1 (docs/plans/procedural_walls.md): a WALL_SPLINE drawn as a polygon of its own kind, its ends attached to a fort
# building's ends (snapped when placed within 4 m), a point marked sharp and back with undo, an end moved onto another building end, and a
# save that keeps the wall (tile_check.sh looks at the saved tile.wce). %TILE% is the kit tile terrain_rugged_hills_desert/1x1/aa.
tile "%TILE%"
frames 5

# A vanilla 8 m straight to attach to: its ends are at x 60 and 70 (the model's x extent, 0 .. 20 scene units, is 10 m in the editor).
add_building barbarian_fort_straight 60 60
frames 60
tile_model_bounds barbarian_fort_straight

# The wall: its first point 1 m off the straight's far end goes onto it; the rest is open ground.
add_polygon "WALL_SPLINE|barbarian_fort_straight|71,61;85,75;100,78;115,72"
frames 2
wall_info
assert_wall 0 - attached free

# A point marked sharp is a corner; undo makes it smooth again.
wall_sharp 1 on
frames 2
assert_wall 1 - attached free
undo
frames 2
assert_wall 0 - attached free

# The last point moved within 4 m of the straight's near end goes onto it. The line now doubles back at point 2: its internal angle is
# under 25 degrees, so it is a corner without being marked.
polygon_point move 3 57 58
frames 2
wall_info
assert_wall 1 - attached attached

save_to "%OUT%/saved"
quit
