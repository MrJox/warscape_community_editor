# Layers (Phase 1 of docs/plans/tile_ted_parity.md): the eye (OIF_VISIBLE) and cursor arrow (OIF_ACTIVE) of a layer hide and lock everything below it, and the structure
# commands (new, rename, move, group, duplicate, delete, export type), each with undo and redo, on a kit tile. %TILE% is osgiliath_test (layers Walls, Fort_Logic, rivers, ...).
tile "%TILE%"
frames 3
tile_tree 1
assert_layer rivers exists 1
# the file itself hides rivers (flags " OIF_ACTIVE") and shows Buildings (" OIF_VISIBLE|OIF_ACTIVE"), as TEd's Scene tab does
assert_flag rivers visible 0
assert_flag rivers active 1
assert_shown rivers 0
assert_flag Buildings visible 1
assert_shown Buildings 1
assert_selectable Buildings 1
assert_modified 0

undo
