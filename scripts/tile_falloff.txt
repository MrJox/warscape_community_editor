# The brushes' falloff curve (docs/plans/workflow_review.md, section 3): a new 2 x 2 tile at density 32 (4 m a pixel, 5 m high in the
# editor); raised for 30 steps (3 m at full weight) with the hard curve (full weight at 0.8 of the radius) and with the linear one (0.2
# there); the curve off again. Run with WCE_TILE_WORKSPACE set to a scratch folder.
new_tile wce_falloff_test 2 2 10 32
frames 5
brush 20 6 0.5
brush_curve hard
paint raise 100 100 30
assert_height 100 100 8 0.05
assert_height 116 100 8 0.05
undo
assert_height 116 100 5 0.01
brush_curve linear
paint raise 100 100 30
assert_height 100 100 8 0.05
assert_height 116 100 5.6 0.05
brush_curve off
quit
