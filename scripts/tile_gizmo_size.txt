# The gizmo at a fixed size on screen (Phase 1 of docs/plans/tile_ted_parity.md): the translate handles of a building on a new tile are
# dragged at 60 and at 120 points (gizmo_drag aims at the handle's tip from the length the viewport drew it with). Run with WCE_TILE_WORKSPACE
# set to a scratch folder; the size goes back to 60 at the end (it is kept in the settings).
new_tile wce_gizmo_test 2 2 10 32
frames 5
add_building barbarian_fort_straight 60 60
frames 5
tile_select BUILDING_INSTANCE
focus
cam 2.2 0.5 40 2
gizmo translate
frames 10
gizmo_size
assert_history 1 1
cam_mark
gizmo_drag z 50
frames 4
assert_history 2 2
assert_cam_same
gizmo_size 120
frames 4
gizmo_drag x -50
frames 4
assert_history 3 3
assert_cam_same
gizmo_size 60
quit
