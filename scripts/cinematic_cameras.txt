# The Cinematic tool's cameras, shots and clip export (docs/plans/cinematic.md, user round 17), run by scripts/cinematic_check.sh
# twice in throw-away settings and projects folders: the sample's units' own animations, its two cameras and the Camera Cuts
# between them, a new camera from the view and a cut to it, and the clip exported a PNG folder a shot with handles.
tool cinematic
layout reset
frames 2
scene_sample
frames 3
cinematic_show Viewport
scene_time 6
frames 30
wait_loaded
frames 10
# A cohort's men have their table's slots (the old fixed list named slots no unit has); a rider his own.
unit_slots 3 500
unit_slots 10 50
# The sample's shots: Camera 1 (track 13), Camera 2 (14) from 5.5 s, Camera 1 again from 8.5 s.
assert_live_camera 4 13
assert_live_camera 6 14
assert_live_camera 9 13
# A camera from the view (track 23), cut to at 10.5 s.
scene_free_camera 1
camera_new
frames 2
camera_cut 23 10.5
assert_live_camera 11 23
assert_live_camera 10 13
# The clip a PNG folder a shot (4 shots), 3 handle frames each side.
scene_export_clip png "%CLIPS%/sample.png" 160 90 shots 3
frames 5
quit
