# The water depth view and Paint water ground types (Phase 4d of docs/plans/tile_ted_parity.md): %TILE% is a kit river tile
# (river/2x2_tl_rt/aa). The view works out the depth from stage B's water map; the river at (240, 64) is over 2 m deep; its deep water
# paint is erased there, and painting the water ground types puts it back; undone. The sea (0 m, over this low tile) and the river ribbons are hidden for the
# capture %OUT%/water_depth.png. Run with WCE_TILE_WORKSPACE set to a scratch folder.
tile "%TILE%"
frames 3
tile_water off
tile_sea off
tile_ground_view water
frames 10
assert_water_depth 240 64 2
brush 10 6 0.5
paint_ground_type none 240 64 1
assert_ground_type 240 64 none
paint_water_types 0.3 2.5
frames 3
assert_ground_type 240 64 "deep water"
cam 0 1.2 200 1
frames 10
shot %OUT%/water_depth.png
frames 2
undo
assert_ground_type 240 64 none
tile_sea on
quit
