# Flip a river's direction (Phase 4c of docs/plans/tile_ted_parity.md): %TILE% is a kit river tile (river/2x2_tl_rt/aa), opened, its river
# flipped once, or twice when %SECOND_FLIP% is `spline_flip` (else empty), and saved into the projects (WCE_TILE_WORKSPACE, a scratch folder). Checked outside: one flip reverses the
# points' world positions and values, two give the river element's bytes back (~/wce_re/scratch/crates_re/py/flipcheck.py did it on
# 2026-10-27).
tile "%TILE%"
frames 3
tile_select SplineRiverInstance
spline_flip
%SECOND_FLIP%
frames 2
save
quit
