# Piece-to-piece snapping (Phase 5g of docs/plans/tile_ted_parity.md; wce's): with "snap to points" on, a fort piece grabbed near another's
# end joins it: it takes the other's rotation and its low end goes onto the other's high end, or its high end onto the other's low end.
# barbarian_fort_straight's frame is 0 .. 20 scene units (10 m) along its x. Run with WCE_TILE_WORKSPACE set to a scratch folder.
new_tile wce_piece_test 2 2 10 32
frames 5
add_building barbarian_fort_straight 60 60
add_building barbarian_fort_straight 90 90
frames 60
tile_select 8
rotate 30
assert_history 3 3
snap vertices on tolerance 0
# Near the first's high end (70, 60): it continues the wall along +x.
grab_to 71 61
assert_world_xz 70 60 0.01
assert_attr selected orientation euler(0,0,0)
assert_history 4 4
# Near the first's low end (60, 60) with its own high end: it runs on along -x.
grab_to 49 59
assert_world_xz 50 60 0.01
# Far from any end: no snapping.
grab_to 100 100
assert_world_xz 100 100 0.01
snap vertices off
quit
